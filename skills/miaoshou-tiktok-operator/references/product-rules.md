# Malaysia Product Rules

## Preferred Starting Themes

Keep the first catalog coherent. Prefer one theme such as:

- Hot-weather outdoor essentials
- Rain-season commuting accessories
- Lightweight sports and walking accessories
- Practical travel organization

## Better First-Test Products

- Quick-dry sock multipacks
- Cooling towels
- Sun sleeves
- Lightweight caps without protected logos
- Reflective running accessories
- Running belts
- Waterproof phone pouches
- Compact rain covers
- Small travel organizers

Use these as directions, not automatic approvals. Verify the actual product, supplier, margins, category, and platform rules.

## Avoid as Initial Traffic Products

- Single very-low-price items whose shipping exceeds product cost
- Generic T-shirts with many sizes and high return risk
- Heavy or bulky goods
- Fragile products
- Products requiring batteries or plugs
- Products making safety, health, waterproof-depth, UV-rating, or performance claims without evidence
- Branded replicas or supplier images containing other shops' identities

## Assortment Guideline

For an initial 20-product catalog:

- 4-6 traffic products
- 8-10 profit products
- 3-4 useful bundles
- 2-4 test products

Traffic products must still have non-negative contribution profit. Use bundles and quantity breaks to avoid losing money on single cheap units.
